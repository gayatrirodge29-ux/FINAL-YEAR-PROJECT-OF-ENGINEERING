Deloitte Off-Campus Hiring

Most hiring in Deloitte happens through:
• Deloitte Off-Campus Drive
• Analyst / Associate hiring programs

🔹 Overall Rounds

  • Online Test
  • Technical Interview
  • Managerial / Case Study Round
  • HR Interview
👉 3–4 rounds depending on role (case study round is common)

🧪 1. Online Test

📌 Sections:

  • Aptitude
    • Quantitative Ability
    • Logical Reasoning
    • Verbal Ability
  • Technical Section
    • Programming basics
    • DBMS
    • OOPs
    • Computer Fundamentals
  • Coding
    • 1–2 questions
    • Medium level

⏱️ Duration: ~2 to 3 hours

💡 Important Points:
  • Focus on problem-solving and logic
  • Coding level moderate
  • Strong verbal skills are important

👨‍💻 2. Technical Interview

  • Tell me about yourself
  • Explain your projects in depth
  • OOPs concepts
  • DBMS and SQL queries
  • Scenario-based technical questions
👉 Focus on practical understanding, not just theory

📊 3. Managerial / Case Study Round

  • Business case problems
  • Situational decision-making
  • Communication and presentation
  • Team and leadership scenarios
👉 You may be asked to solve real-world business problems

🧾 4. HR Interview

  • Why Deloitte?
  • Willingness to relocate
  • Strengths & weaknesses
  • Career goals
  • Salary discussion
👉 Checks confidence, communication & professionalism

💼 Roles Offered

Role Package (Approx)
Analyst ~4–6 LPA
Associate Analyst ~5–7 LPA
Consultant (entry level) ~7–10 LPA
