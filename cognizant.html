Cognizant Off-Campus Hiring

Most hiring in Cognizant happens through:
• Cognizant Off-Campus Drive
• GenC Hiring Program (Generation Cognizant)

🔹 Overall Rounds

  • Online Test
  • Technical Interview
  • HR Interview
👉 Usually 2–3 rounds (sometimes managerial is included in technical)

🧪 1. Online Test

📌 Sections:

  • Aptitude
    • Quantitative Ability
    • Logical Reasoning
    • Verbal Ability
  • Technical Section
    • Programming basics
    • DBMS
    • OOPs
    • Basic Computer Fundamentals
  • Coding
    • 1–2 questions
    • Easy to Medium level

⏱️ Duration: ~2 to 2.5 hours

💡 Important Points:
  • Aptitude + Communication are important
  • Coding level is easier than Infosys
  • Focus on basics

👨‍💻 2. Technical Interview

  • Tell me about yourself
  • Explain your projects
  • OOPs concepts
  • DBMS basics
  • Simple coding questions
👉 Mostly checks fundamentals + communication

🧾 3. HR Interview

  • Why Cognizant?
  • Willingness to relocate
  • Strengths & weaknesses
  • Career goals
👉 Focus is on attitude and communication
