Infosys Off-Campus Hiring

Most hiring in Infosys happens through:
• Infosys Off-Campus Drive
• InfyTQ (Infosys Talent Next)

🔹 Overall Rounds

  • Online Test
  • Technical Interview
  • Managerial Round (sometimes combined)
  • HR Interview
👉 All rounds are elimination rounds

🧪 1. Online Test

📌 Sections:

  • Aptitude
    • Quantitative Ability
    • Logical Reasoning
    • Verbal Ability
  • Technical Section
    • Programming (C / Java / Python)
    • DBMS
    • OS
    • OOPs
  • Coding Test
    • 2–3 questions
    • 1 easy, 1–2 medium

⏱️ Duration: 2.5 to 3 hours

💡 Important Points:
  • Aptitude cutoff is very strict
  • Coding level slightly higher than TCS
  • Questions are pattern-based

👨‍💻 2. Technical Interview

  • Tell me about yourself
  • Explain your projects
  • OOPs concepts
  • DBMS (normalization, joins)
  • OS basics
  • Write simple code (string/array)
👉 You may need to write code on paper or verbally

👔 3. Managerial Round

  • Team handling ability
  • Internship / project discussion
  • Situational questions
  • Work pressure handling

🧾 4. HR Interview

  • Why Infosys?
  • Will you relocate?
  • Bond-related questions
  • Strengths & weaknesses
  • Career goals
👉 Focus is on behavior + commitment
