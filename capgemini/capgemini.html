Capgemini Off-Campus Hiring

Most hiring in Capgemini happens through:
• Capgemini Off-Campus Drive
• Pooled campus / Superset hiring

🔹 Overall Rounds

  • Online Test
  • Technical Interview
  • HR Interview
👉 Usually 2–3 rounds (technical + managerial often combined)

🧪 1. Online Test

📌 Sections:

  • Aptitude
    • Quantitative Ability
    • Logical Reasoning
    • Verbal Ability
  • Pseudo Code / Programming
    • Code tracing
    • Output-based questions
  • Coding
    • 1–2 questions
    • Easy to Medium level
  • English Communication
    • Grammar
    • Sentence correction

⏱️ Duration: ~2 to 2.5 hours

💡 Important Points:
  • Pseudo code is very important
  • Aptitude + English have separate cutoffs
  • Coding is moderate level

👨‍💻 2. Technical Interview

  • Tell me about yourself
  • Explain your projects
  • OOPs concepts
  • DBMS basics
  • Simple coding / logic questions
👉 Focus on fundamentals and clarity

🧾 3. HR Interview

  • Why Capgemini?
  • Willingness to relocate
  • Strengths & weaknesses
  • Career goals
👉 Communication skills are very important
